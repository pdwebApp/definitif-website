Our Offerings

Mutual Funds

Mutual Funds are professionally managed investment vehicles that pool money from multiple investors to invest in equities, debt instruments, money market securities, or a combination of these. Managed by Asset Management Companies (AMCs), they offer diversification, liquidity, transparency, and regulatory oversight under Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). Mutual funds are suitable for investors seeking systematic wealth creation, income generation, or capital preservation based on their risk profile.

Specialized Investment Funds (SIF)

Specialized Investment Funds are focused investment products designed to invest in specific themes, sectors, strategies, or asset classes. They may follow concentrated, tactical, or strategy-driven mandates such as long-short equity, sector-specific exposure, or structured strategies. These funds are generally meant for informed investors seeking differentiated opportunities beyond traditional diversified mutual funds.

Portfolio Management Services (PMS)

Portfolio Management Services (PMS) provide customized investment management for high-net-worth investors. Under PMS, investments are made in the client’s own Demat account, offering direct ownership of securities. PMS strategies may include equity-focused, debt-focused, or hybrid approaches, and are tailored according to individual risk appetite, return expectations, and financial goals.

Alternative Investment Funds (AIF)

Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) are privately pooled investment vehicles regulated by Securities and Exchange Board of India. They invest in alternative asset classes such as private equity, venture capital, hedge funds, real estate, infrastructure, distressed assets, and structured credit. AIFs are categorized into Category I, II, and III based on their investment strategy and regulatory framework, and are generally suitable for sophisticated investors with higher risk tolerance.

Pure Term Insurance

Pure Term Insurance is a life insurance product that provides financial protection to the nominee in case of the policyholder’s untimely demise during the policy term. It offers high coverage at relatively low premiums and does not include any maturity or investment component. Term insurance is essential for income replacement, liability protection, and long-term family financial security.

Health Insurance

Health Insurance provides coverage against hospitalization expenses, medical treatments, surgeries, and critical illnesses. It helps individuals and families manage rising healthcare costs and ensures access to quality medical care without financial strain. Policies may include individual, family floater, critical illness, and senior citizen plans.

Corporate Fixed Deposits (Corporate FDs)

Corporate Fixed Deposits are term deposits offered by companies to raise funds from investors. They typically offer higher interest rates than bank fixed deposits but carry relatively higher credit risk. Investors should evaluate the company’s credit rating, financial strength, and repayment history before investing.

Corporate Bonds

Corporate Bonds are debt securities issued by companies to raise capital. Investors lend money to the issuing company in exchange for periodic interest payments and repayment of principal at maturity. Corporate bonds may offer higher yields compared to government bonds, depending on credit quality and market conditions.

Government Bonds

Government Bonds are debt instruments issued by central or state governments to finance public expenditure. In India, these are issued by the Government of India and state governments and are considered among the safest investment options due to sovereign backing. They provide fixed or floating interest payments and are suitable for conservative investors seeking stability and predictable income.
