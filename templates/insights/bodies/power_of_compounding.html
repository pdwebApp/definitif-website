Compounding is one of the most powerful ideas in long-term investing. It allows the returns generated by your investments to generate further returns over time, helping even regular contributions grow into a meaningful corpus.

The key ingredient is time. The earlier you begin investing and the longer you remain invested, the more opportunity your money has to benefit from compounding.

Key Takeaway

Compounding rewards consistency and patience. Starting early and investing regularly can make a meaningful difference to long-term wealth creation.

How Compounding Works

When you invest, your money may generate returns. Over time, those returns can also generate returns of their own. This creates a cycle in which your investment can grow at an increasing pace, provided you remain invested for a sufficiently long period.

For example, if you invest ₹10,000 every month and your investment earns an assumed annualised return of 12%, the value of your portfolio does not grow only because of your monthly contributions. It also grows because earlier contributions have had more time to compound.

The Impact of Time: An Illustrative Example

Consider an investor who invests ₹10,000 every month and earns an assumed annualised return of 12%. The table below illustrates how the estimated corpus can change with different investment periods.

Why Time Matters

  • Returns on Returns: Compounding allows investment returns to generate further returns over time, potentially accelerating portfolio growth in later years.
  • Longer Investment Horizon: A longer horizon gives investments more time to recover from short-term market fluctuations and participate in long-term growth.
  • Consistency Matters: Regular investing can help build discipline and ensure that money continues to work towards long-term goals.
  • Small Contributions Can Grow: Beginning with an affordable amount may be more effective than waiting for a larger amount to invest later.
  • Patience is Important: Compounding is gradual in the early years and becomes more visible as the investment horizon extends.

Four Ways to Make Compounding Work for You

  1. 01

    Start Early

    Begin investing as soon as possible, even if the initial amount is small. Time in the market can be an important advantage.

  2. 02

    Invest Regularly

    Use systematic investments to build a consistent investing habit and participate in long-term wealth creation.

  3. 03

    Stay Invested

    Avoid making investment decisions based solely on short-term market movements. A long-term approach can help compounding work over time.

  4. 04

    Review Periodically

    Review your goals, risk tolerance and portfolio allocation as your financial circumstances change.

Let Time Work in Your Favour

Compounding is not about finding a perfect investment or timing the market. It is about giving your investments sufficient time to grow while remaining consistent with your plan.

Start early, invest regularly and remain invested for the long term.
