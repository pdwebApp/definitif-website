Retirement may seem like a distant milestone, particularly when you are young and focused on your career, family and other financial priorities. However, one of the most valuable decisions you can make for your financial future is to start planning for retirement early.

The biggest advantage of starting early is the power of compounding. When you invest regularly, your investments generate returns, and those returns can generate further returns over time. The longer your money remains invested, the greater the potential impact of compounding.

Time is Your Greatest Investment Advantage

Starting early allows you to build wealth gradually, benefit from compounding and avoid the pressure of making significantly larger investments later in life.

The Cost of Waiting: An Illustrative Example

Consider two individuals who want to build a retirement corpus by the age of 60. Both invest the same amount every month and earn an assumed annualised return of 12%. The only difference is when they start investing.

Illustration: Assuming a constant annualised return of 12%, monthly investments of ₹10,000 and retirement at age 60. Calculations assume monthly compounding and investments made at the end of each month. Actual market returns are not guaranteed and will fluctuate.

Why Starting Early Makes a Difference

  • Power of Compounding: Your accumulated returns generate additional returns over time, helping your wealth grow over longer investment horizons.
  • Lower Investment Burden: Starting early allows you to accumulate a larger corpus with relatively smaller monthly contributions compared with someone who starts investing later.
  • Better Ability to Manage Market Volatility: A longer investment horizon can help investors navigate short-term market fluctuations without making decisions based solely on temporary market movements.
  • Financial Independence: A well-planned retirement corpus can help meet future living expenses, healthcare costs and lifestyle requirements without depending entirely on employment income or family support.
  • Protection Against Inflation: Retirement planning should account for the rising cost of living. Starting early provides more time to build an investment corpus capable of supporting future purchasing power.

Five Steps Towards a Secure Retirement

  1. 01

    Start Early

    Begin investing as soon as you start earning, even if the initial amount is small.

  2. 02

    Invest Regularly

    Use systematic investments to develop financial discipline and participate in long-term wealth creation.

  3. 03

    Increase Contributions

    Gradually increase your investments as your income grows to keep pace with your retirement goals.

  4. 04

    Diversify Your Portfolio

    Maintain an appropriate mix of equity, debt and other suitable investments based on your age, goals and risk tolerance.

  5. 05

    Review and Rebalance

    Periodically review your retirement plan and adjust your investments as your financial circumstances change.

Your Future Self Will Thank You

Retirement planning is not about how much you earn today, but how effectively you prepare for tomorrow. The earlier you begin, the more time you give your investments to grow.

Don't wait for the perfect time or a larger income. Start early, invest consistently and let time work in your favour.
