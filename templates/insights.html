Indian Stock Market Trends

The equity market is consolidating under pressure from global tensions. Indices remain range-bound, reflecting cautious sentiment amid rising crude prices and rupee weakness.

  • Market Sentiment: Fragile, volatility driven by geopolitics.
  • Sector Trends: IT and financials weak; energy, metals, and select mid-caps show resilience.
  • Institutional Flows: FIIs continue heavy selling; DIIs provide partial support.
  • Global Influence: US–Iran conflict, crude above $105, and currency lows dominate.
  • Outlook: Data-dependent with risks from inflation, RBI policy, and geopolitical escalation.

Overall, markets are consolidating with stock-specific opportunities, especially in commodity-linked and mid-cap segments.

Indian Bond Market Trends

The bond market has turned cautious as the 10‑year government bond yield crossed 7%, driven by inflation concerns and global tensions.

  • Yield Movement: 10‑year yield above 7%, signaling rising borrowing costs.
  • Auction Demand: Participation remains steady, though higher yields reflect investor caution.
  • Liquidity Conditions: Institutional buying continues but is offset by inflationary pressures.
  • Policy Watch: Crude‑driven inflation and rupee weakness keep RBI policy under close scrutiny.
  • Investment View: Fixed income still relevant for conservative investors, but duration risk has increased.

Overall, yields have firmed in response to geopolitical shocks and imported inflation, making bonds a defensive but more yield‑sensitive component of diversified portfolios.
